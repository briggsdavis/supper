# Scenes and Supper: V1 pages and flows

## Public pages

- `/` Home: hero, next dinner, upcoming dinners, featured recipes, alerts signup.
- `/events` Upcoming dinners: poster, title, date, venue, price, seats left.
- `/events/:slug` Dinner detail: synopsis, date/time/venue, menu, price, buy button, comments.
- `/titles` Recipe browser: search movies and shows, browse grid.
- `/titles/:slug` Title page: poster, synopsis, recipes, linked dinners, comments.
- `/recipes/:slug` Recipe: embedded video (Babish when available), ingredients, steps, source link.
- `/login` Sign in / sign up.
- `/account` My tickets, alert preferences.
- `/checkout/success` Order confirmation.

## Admin pages

- `/admin/events` Create, edit, publish dinners.
- `/admin/titles` Add movies and shows.
- `/admin/recipes` Add recipes, attach to titles.
- `/admin/events/:id/guests` Attendee list for check-in.
- `/admin/comments` Hide or delete comments.

## User flows

1. Buy a ticket: Home or Events > Dinner detail > Buy > sign in > Stripe Checkout > success page + email > ticket shows in Account.
2. Find recipes: Titles > search > Title page > Recipe > watch video.
3. Get alerts: sign up from Home or Account > new dinners send an email.
4. Comment: Dinner or Title page > sign in > post what you want to see or eat.

## Admin flows

1. New dinner: add title > create dinner (date, venue, price, capacity, menu) > publish.
2. Add recipes: pick title > add recipe (video URL, ingredients, steps).
3. Night of: open guest list > check people in.
