# Scenes and Supper: kickoff notes (Nov 19, 2026)

Source: SS Web Dev Team Sync with Devon Colebank.

## Concept

- Watch a movie and eat a meal themed to it.
- Local take on the market leader Devon shared ("Work and Film"-style).
- Domain: scenesandsupper.com (Devon bought it).
- Nate and Max get equity, similar to Strike.

## Deadline

- First event: Dec 19. Tickets must be on sale before then.
- Devon wants a V1 by end of next week (around Nov 27).

## V1 features

- Home page.
- Events/tickets page: upcoming screenings for the rest of this year into next year.
- Event listing: movie synopsis, menu, buy ticket (payment processing).
- Member login, so we can build a user list and send alerts about new shows.
- Comments on listings: what people want to see and eat from the movie.
- Recipes: search a movie or show, land on its page, see recipes.
  - Mainly Binging with Babish, with the video embedded.
  - Plus general recipes (can be AI-gathered).
  - Recipes exist for titles even if there is no dinner for them.
- Admin side to add movies, events, and recipes.

## Look and feel

- Chromatic RGB / CRT feel, like being very close to a screen.
- Tasteful, not too much. Not futuristic.
- Some texture.
- Idea: the RGB effect applied to a fork and knife.
